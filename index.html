How to read the Energy Rating Label

Two numbers on the label matter most: the star rating and the yearly energy use.

Stars compare like with like
More stars means an appliance uses less electricity than others of the same type and a similar size. Stars are most useful when you are choosing between two models.
kWh per year is the number to use
The label estimates energy use in kilowatt-hours per year, based on a standard test. Multiply it by your electricity price to get an approximate yearly running cost.
Your habits change the result
The label assumes 10 hours of viewing a day. If you watch more or less, your real use will differ. The calculator lets you enter your own hours.
